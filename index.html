About

Bekmas is Thomas and Bekah Poyser and family, from the Annapolis Valley in Nova Scotia. Everything here is built at home, in the evenings, around raising five kids — software made by a family, for families and the churches they belong to.

The name on the door frame is “The Poyser Family.” Bekmas is just the name on the apps.
